01 — About

Good testing starts with a clear question.

What should this feature do, and what happens when it does not? I enjoy answering that by understanding the expected behavior, exploring the edges, and writing down what I find so another person can reproduce it.

I am building these skills through my DEPI training and hands-on practice with sample applications. The case studies on this site are practice work, and each one is labeled that way.

Honest scope: I am early in my career. This portfolio shows how I think, plan and document testing, not years of industry experience.

How I Approach Testing

  1. Understand the requirement.
  2. Identify positive, negative, and boundary scenarios.
  3. Design clear and reusable test cases.
  4. Execute tests and record actual results.
  5. Document defects with reproducible steps.
  6. Retest fixes and perform relevant regression checks.
