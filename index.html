01 / THE INVESTIGATOR'S MINDSET

TEST THE CLAIM.
TRACE THE TRUTH.

A feature working once is not proof that it works correctly. Testing means examining behavior, conditions, and risk.

Don't trust the screen.
Question the behavior.

I start by understanding what a feature is supposed to do. Then I explore normal use, invalid inputs, boundary values, and situations that are easy to overlook.

When behavior looks wrong, I aim to reproduce it, compare expected and actual results, and document the evidence clearly enough to support investigation.

BUG-001 / FUNCTIONAL ILLUSTRATIVE REPORT

Login form submits without required-field feedback

PROPOSED SEVERITY: MAJOR FAIL

Submitting the login form while both fields are empty does not provide clear field-level validation feedback.

1. Open the login page.

2. Leave the email and password fields empty.

3. Click the Sign in button.

Required-field validation is displayed, and invalid data is not submitted.

No clear validation message appears after submission.
